      How it scores. Each hour starts with light (dawn, dusk or night, depending on the species), then adds the pressure trend, temperature, wind and rain, then a small bonus when a moon period overlaps. The moon is worth at most 13 of 100 points because studies of deer and fish disagree on whether it matters. If you mark which winds work for your stand, whitetail and hog scores also lose 10 points when the wind is from another side. Pressure and temperature weights are educated guesses, so use the log to check them. Fish scores use air temperature, not water temperature.
